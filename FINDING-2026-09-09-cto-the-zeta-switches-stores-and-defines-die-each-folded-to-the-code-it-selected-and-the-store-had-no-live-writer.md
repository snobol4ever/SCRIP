## Landing 3 — the derivation (one function, language-blind)

`fl_derive_tier(g)` in `frame_layout.c`, at the head of `ir_drive_slot_assign`, so every graph in every language is derived before its slots are planned, at compile time and at run time (asserted Prolog clauses included). It reads the graph's boxes only. WINDOW: a box that leaves at γ and is re-entered at β with unbounded growth between (SUSPEND, the SCAN family, TO/TO_BY/LIMIT/REPALT/ITERATE, PROC_GEN, CREATE/ACTIVATE/CORET/COFAIL, DISJUNCTION/GALT/CUT/PATTERN_ALT, CALL_BUILTIN_GEN/KW_ICON_GEN/CALL_VALUE, the reversible assigns, and the match boxes whose body owns a frame). CALLEE: a box that hands control to an activation that owns its own frame (the CALL family, GOTO_DEFERRED, INDIRECT_GOTO, MATCH). A graph carrying statement boxes is a statement chain — the standing frame of a program or a DEFINE body whose statements carve per-box activation frames, the match regime — and is left as lowered. A root graph is the standing frame. Otherwise the graph is a HOST iff WINDOW or CALLEE, and both frame flags follow the host: condition two names the host that pins itself (CEO-380 generalised), condition one is implied wherever a window or a callee can separate a result from its operator. The per-language assignments (`lower_prolog.c` ×3, `lower_pascal.c`, `lower_raku.c`) and `icn_host_pinned()`'s shape test are deleted; the Icon host pin reads the derived flag, keeping only the generator exclusion (a suspended generator's frame is carved at the call by N-3, not by the host prologue). hq_U's objection, taken: a SNOBOL4 program or DEFINE body is ONE graph, the statement chain, and its ARBNO host is boxes inside it, so the statement-chain branch catches it first and the derivation leaves the SNOBOL4 match regime as lowered; hq_U's ARBNO host is NOT pinned by this derivation, its host-pins-itself cure stays diagnosed-not-built, and hq_U's gdb walk (rbp climbing one activation per lap, r14 stuck at 1, the fault on the arm's first instruction) is the concrete form of condition two the match regime must be made to satisfy next.
